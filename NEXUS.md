## Full description (Nexus BBCode)

[b]Mage Balance — an early-access spell rebalance for Gothic 1 Remake.[/b]

Gothic 1 Remake's mages fall off hard — several spells are laughably weak by mid/late game. This mod rebalances spell damage (and a few other stats) at runtime. [b]No game files are modified[/b] — everything is changed in memory and reverts when you close the game, and every spell is configurable in one tidy file.

[b]⚠ Early version — please read[/b]
This is an early release, pushed on purpose to gather feedback. Expect frequent changes and a long to-do list — but the most important fixes are already in: a properly devastating [b]Fire Rain[/b] and a stronger [b]Fireball[/b]. Tell me what feels off and it'll get tuned.

[b]Current changes[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Fireball / Feuerball[/b] — +100% damage (x2), all charge levels
[*][b]Fire Rain / Feuerregen[/b] — +150% damage (x2.5) and a much bigger rain area
[*][b]Ice Arrow / Eispfeil[/b] — raised to Firebolt parity, so it's worth casting again
[*][b]Death Breath / Todeshauch[/b] — +100% damage (x2)
[*][b]Pyrokinesis / Pyrokinese[/b] — +150% damage (x2.5)
[*]Firebolt and Ball Lightning left at vanilla (already fine)
[/list]

[b]Planned / open to-dos[/b]
[list]
[*]Lightning (Blitz) buff — needs a different approach (it's a beam, not a projectile)
[*]Cheaper magic-circle learning & smoother mage progression
[*]Faster casting/charging as your magic circle rises
[*]Light (Licht) available at novice tier, early — a great early-game mage pickup
[*]Possible merge with the mage robe-values mod
[*]More spells and fine-tuning based on your feedback
[/list]

[b]Configurable[/b]
One readable block per spell in [i]config.lua[/i]: a simple damage multiplier (or exact per-magic-circle values), plus optional stat tweaks (AoE area, etc.). Adding or adjusting a spell takes seconds — no code editing.

[*]Drop the [i]G1R_MageBalance[/i] folder into [i]...\Gothic 1 Remake\G1R\Binaries\Win64\Mods\[/i]
[/list]
